# Naming the public product

The name should signal **reliable engineering judgment across AI tools**, not simply a collection of coding prompts. We have not renamed the public repository, command paths or GitHub links while the product identity is undecided.

## Positioning

> Your engineering judgment system, carried across coding agents. It learns from verified experiences, keeps personal knowledge private, and offers community-reviewed improvements without mixing users' rules.

## Naming criteria

- Short enough to remember and say aloud
- Recognizable as engineering/proof/learning infrastructure, not a generic chatbot
- Flexible enough for UX/UI and product research, not only debugging
- No implied guarantee of autonomous verification or model training
- Check existing software, trademarks, GitHub names and relevant domains before choosing

### Directions worth exploring

**ProofMesa:** evokes a common place for evidence-based engineering decisions. **SkillLedger:** emphasizes the record of what was learned and why. **RulePath:** emphasizes choosing and following appropriate guidance.

These are naming **candidates only**, not cleared brands, available domains or trademarks. Better not to choose the final public name until licensing, private profiles, public onboarding and a user-facing demo are working.

## Product promise

Someone without write access to this repository can apply a neutral set of engineering practices to their own project. Their private lessons remain theirs. Only verified, reviewed and appropriately licensed general improvements can become public shared guidance.
