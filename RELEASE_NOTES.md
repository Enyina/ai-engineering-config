# What's new

## October 17, 2026

**More understandable change descriptions**

Every saved change must now have a short headline that makes sense without technical knowledge. The headline should say what improved rather than listing programming details. Projects with special formatting requirements will still follow those requirements.


**Clearer explanations of completed work**

We've improved the shared guidance for personal projects. From now on, completed changes should be saved with a clear description, and updates should explain the difference they make in everyday language.

These instructions have been saved for future use. They have not changed the tools already installed on your computer.

**Older guidance now matches**

We have also updated older written instructions so they no longer give mixed advice about saving completed work. Future updates should explain changes in the same simple way.

**Better suggestions for designs and code**

Personal projects can now compare different approaches instead of following one familiar solution. Suggestions explain the benefits, drawbacks and what to check before choosing.

We've also added a library of trusted public references and a regular check for possible changes. New findings will be reviewed before becoming permanent guidance.

These improvements are saved in the shared personal guidance. They do not automatically redesign your projects or change your computer's settings.


## October 17, 2026 — Portfolio review lessons

**Clearer evidence before showcasing work**

We've recorded a new review method for checking personal websites against actual career history. It helps distinguish finished work from prototypes and highlights where claims need proof.

**Your approval stays central**

Suggested changes to showcased projects must be explained before anything is hidden, replaced, or rewritten. The new method is waiting for review and has not changed the public website.

**More useful advice at work**

The system can now suggest relevant approaches even when a workplace has no specific instruction for a task. Every suggestion shows where the idea came from, and workplace requirements still come first. Nothing is copied into a workplace automatically.

**More frequent research checks**

Approved public references will now be checked twice a day instead of once a week. Any possible changes are flagged for review before affecting future guidance.
